Cartographic Relief terrain credits

FrameFlow renders elevation colors and simulated sunlight from the Mapzen Terrain Tiles dataset. This is a styled derivative of elevation data. It contains no satellite photography, roads or labels.

Ocean depths use ETOPO1 where supplied by the tiles. Source resolution varies geographically; zooming further does not create new measurements. The renderer uses local slope lighting and subtle curvature shading, rather than cast shadows or a three-dimensional terrain mesh.

World c. AD 1300

Historical territories: Cliopatria v0.2.0, Seshat Global History Databank, Bennett et al. (2025), Scientific Data, licensed under CC BY 4.0. FrameFlow selects polity records whose inclusive date range contains AD 1300, excludes relation records, quantizes coordinates to 0.0001 degrees, orders territories by area, and annotates parent realm names. No historical corrections have been applied. The bundled snapshot and its source metadata are in world-1300.json; the reproducible build script is build-historical-1300.py.

Boundaries are approximate representations of recorded historical extents; realms and their constituent polities can overlap. Plain land means the snapshot has no recorded polity there, and does not mean it was uninhabited or unclaimed. Natural Earth modern coastlines provide geographic context; shorelines are not reconstructed for AD 1300. Include the Cliopatria attribution and license link with published exports that use this map.
