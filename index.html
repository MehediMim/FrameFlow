Everything a studio needs.

Shared by all four studios.
◫

Frame-based timeline

Scrub, retime and trim on a real frame ruler. Space plays, arrows step one frame, Shift jumps ten.

◇

Keyframes & easing

Key any property, pick easing per keyframe, or turn on auto-key and just move things.

↶

Undo, redo, shortcuts

Ctrl/Cmd+Z and Shift+Z, plus K to key a transform. Mistakes are cheap.

⌂

Saves on your device

Your latest project is kept in the browser. Download a project file for a portable backup.

▶

Video and PNG export

Record WebM (or MP4 where the browser allows) and export any frame as a clean PNG.

∅

No account, no backend

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript in your browser. Nothing to install, nothing to sign up for.
